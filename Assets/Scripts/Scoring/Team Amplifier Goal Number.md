# Team Amplifier goal badge

Open **MASSIVE > Shared Settings > Amplifier > Goal Multiplier Badge** (also available through **MASSIVE > Amplifier > Shared Settings**).

The team multiplier appears only after its first Amplifier capture. It retains the original small black number over a white disc over the animated rainbow energy core. The complete badge is centered vertically at the goal mouth: the Light goal keeps the left half, and the Dark goal keeps the right half. The mask is anchored to the goal, independently of the size and movement of the score-void mass. The previous HUD location is suppressed by the existing team presenter.

Each entry in `Level Scales` (×2, ×4 and ×8) has `Whole Scale`, `White Circle Scale` and `Text Scale`. Whole Scale sets the complete badge diameter as a fraction of the goal diameter; the circle diameter and text height are proportions of that badge. `Overall Scale` is a master adjustment for all levels and layers. Existing global circle/text proportions migrate to every level to preserve authored tuning. Add entries if the scoring economy adds more multiplier levels. Zero placement offsets bisect the badge; `Outward Offset` moves it farther into the goal, and `Vertical Offset` moves it within the ring. All layers share the same ring mask. Font, prefix, team number colors, white-disc color, number outline and ring inset are shared across scenes. A blank prefix shows only the numeral.

Use the ×1/×2/×4/×8 buttons above the settings for an edit-mode scene preview. `Stop preview` or closing Shared Settings removes it. Preview state never changes team scores or overrides Play Mode.

The `Animate 0 → ×2`, `Animate ×2 → ×4` and `Animate ×4 → ×8` buttons preview capture transitions. Under `Multiplier Transitions`, `Growth Seconds` and `Growth Easing` control interpolation of each level's authored sizes and text offset. First capture grows the core and circle from zero, keeps text absent until both reach their targets, then reveals the text by growing its SDF stroke thickness to the font's default. `First Text Grow Seconds` and `First Text Grow Easing` tune this second part. Subsequent captures use the exact team-score `DIGIT_MORPH` preset and intensity from Shared Settings > Text. Its temporary glyphs share the goal mask. `Animate Changes` disables transitions without changing the authored sizes. Match reset hides the badge immediately; consecutive captures continue growth from the currently rendered size.

Each `Level Scales` entry also has one `Text Horizontal Offset`, as a fraction of that level's badge diameter. Negative moves Light left and Dark right (farther into the visible goal halves); positive reverses both. Zero centers the text on the badge. Dark always mirrors Light. The circle, rainbow core and clipping border stay fixed. Existing settings seed each level from the previously authored Light offset, while preserving the per-level scales.

The existing `TeamAmplifierPresenter` subscribes to `MatchScoreService`; no new score authority or scene migration is required. It resolves its team's `ScoreVoidMetaballsVisual` in the same scene, or accepts an explicit local binding. Generated objects/materials are transient and cleaned up on disable. The badge reuses the old HUD core's actual sphere and energy material, with no shell, colliders or gameplay scripts. Goal clipping and transparent draw states are applied only to its material instance; physical cores retain opaque depth-writing defaults. The core, white disc and text draw in that order after opaque score mass and before the transparent ring/border. A shared antialiased goal-space mask clips both the arena side and the inside edge of the ring. COSMOS uses its elliptical cap instead of a circle. Scenes without a supported score-goal container omit the badge.

Editor validation: `Massive.EditorTools.TeamAmplifierGoalNumberValidation.Run(outputFolder)` checks both presenters, base-state hiding, level sizes, alignment, clipping direction, shader/layer setup, shared settings preservation and preview cleanup, and captures all three levels.

`RunTransitions(outputFolder)` additionally checks the delayed SDF reveal, shared score morph, clipping of temporary glyphs, exact destination scales, interruption and preview cancellation. `RunRuntime()` checks scoring and reset wiring in a disposable Play Mode match.
